Air Elite® by World Fuel adds locations in Bangkok and Augusta sgrecco Fri, 03/20/2026 - 12:32 Aviation Mar 23, 2026 4 minutes World Fuel has added MJets Company Limited in Thailand and Augusta Regional Airport Aviation Services in Augusta, Georgia, to the Air Elite® by World Fuel network, expanding global coverage for business aviation operators. Press Release RELEASE DATE: March 23, 2026 CONTACT Peter Stevens, PStevens@world-kinect.com March 23, 2026 – HOUSTON – World Fuel announced the addition of MJets Company Limited in Thailand and Augusta Regional Airport Aviation Services in Augusta, Georgia, to Air Elite® by World Fuel (Air Elite) further expanding the global Air Elite network serving business aviation worldwide. Air Elite establishes shared service and safety standards for its member locations, emphasizing consistent service culture, adherence to industry-recognized safety certifications, and participation in World Fuel programs designed to enhance the ground experience for operators and passengers. MJets Company Limited, based in Bangkok, joins Air Elite as one of Southeast Asia’s most established private aviation service providers, supporting international operators across Thailand and the broader region. The company provides comprehensive private aviation services including charter, aircraft management, ground handling, and maintenance. “This alignment reflects how MJets operates today and how we are building for scale and continuity,” said Natthapatr Sibunruang, chief executive officer of MJets Company Limited. “By working within globally standardized service and safety frameworks, MJets reinforces its position as a reliable regional gateway, supporting international operators with reliable execution and operational peace of mind as business aviation activities across Southeast Asia continue to evolve.” As the first Thai aviation organization to be certified with both IS-BAO and IS-BAH, MJets has established itself as a leading provider of business aviation services in the region. Augusta Regional Airport Aviation Services, located at Augusta Regional Airport (KAGS) in Augusta, Georgia, joins the Air Elite Network as a key gateway serving business aviation traffic to the Central Savannah River Area. Positioned centrally between the main terminal and the air traffic control tower, the facility is located about 20 minutes from Augusta National Golf Club, host of the Masters Tournament. The facility offers a well-appointed executive terminal with conference rooms and lounge areas, a spacious 30-acre ramp, and NATA-qualified line technicians supporting a wide range of aircraft. Operators benefit from guaranteed quick turns of 30 minutes or less, efficient ramp access, and full fuel support, including Jet A and Avgas, as well as maintenance, avionics, and catering services. “This membership marks an important milestone in our ongoing commitment to excellence as we collaborate to elevate service standards across the industry,” said Kenneth Hinkle, director of aviation services. “We look forward to a long-lasting partnership built on mutual growth and active engagement.” “The addition of MJets and Augusta Regional Airport Aviation Services strengthens the Air Elite Network in two important markets and expands the global footprint available to operators,” said Malcolm Hawkins, senior vice president, global business aviation, World Fuel. “For flight departments and crews, Air Elite locations deliver a consistent standard of service and operational expertise across the network.” Operators visiting both locations will have access to World Fuel products and services, including Contract Fuel, AVCARD® by World Fuel, World Fuel Trip Support, and World Fuel Rewards. *** About Air Elite® by World Fuel Air Elite® by World Fuel is the global FBO network curated for elite service. Each location is committed to service excellence, is audited to ensure the highest facility standards, undergoes industry-leading Air Elite Service Culture training, and awards bonus World Fuel Rewards globally. The premier network of locations is strategically positioned around the world. For more information, visit www.airelitenetwork.com .
