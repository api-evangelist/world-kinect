Company news Industry news The AIN FBO Survey is widely regarded as one of the most respected benchmarks in business aviation, and for the 45th consecutive year, it has highlighted the very best in fixed‑base operations worldwide. Based on direct feedback from pilots, flight departments, and operators, the survey recognizes those FBOs that deliver exceptional performance across all five evaluation categories, from customer service and facilities to line operations and professionalism. Earning top rankings is no small feat; it requires a sustained commitment to quality, consistency, and excellence in an increasingly competitive global market. Following this year’s announcement, we congratulate our World Fuel and Air Elite® by World Fuel network members who have been recognized among the industry’s top performers. Their achievements reflect not only outstanding operational standards but also the dedication of their teams, who continue to raise the bar for business aviation services. Top-rated FBOs in the Americas by overall average We are delighted to recognize our network locations ranked in the top 5%, 10%, and 20% of the AIN FBO Survey. This year’s results include an exceptional top‑five overall finish for Henriksen Jet Center at Austin Executive Airport (KEDC), Texas, and a milestone first‑time placement in the top 5% for StanCraft Jet Center at Coeur d’Alene Airport – Pappy Boyington Field (KCOE), Idaho, reflecting consistently outstanding performance as judged by those who use these facilities firsthand.
